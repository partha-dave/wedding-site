Martinstown House
The Curragh, County Kildare, Ireland · 14–15 August 2027

Martinstown House is a family-operated country house set in the leafy landscape of the Curragh in County Kildare. It’s set on a fully working farmyard that houses cattle, sheep, horses and two rescue donkeys, and the entire estate is a bird sanctuary.
